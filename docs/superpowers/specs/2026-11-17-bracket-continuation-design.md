# Complete bracket continuation

The reader's requested layout is the design: show the full route from the
first round to the final, with all future match boxes and pale directional
connections present from the start. Keep confirmed progress stronger. Put the
third-place match below the final as a separate semifinal-loser branch.

For SCC this is eight Round of 16 cards, four quarterfinals, two semifinals,
one final and one third-place card. The final immediately follows semifinals
horizontally; third place shares the final's column below it. A winner filling
a card must not move that card or any later round.

Official seed order determines the planned routes. Planned routes are separate
from participant advancement evidence: drawing a future arrow never declares
a winner. Real published pairings take their reserved slots; the remaining
slots stay visible. Conflicting pairings must not be routed by guessing.

Future names, surfaces and borders are muted while remaining readable in both
themes. Future cards have no invented score or game action. The confirmed
Nakamura qualification remains visible. Each connection has an arrowhead;
unconfirmed routes are pale, confirmed routes stronger, and focused confirmed
routes use the existing accent.

The shared Dart model and layout feed phone and desktop. The website follows
the same seed and continuation contract. Validation covers full future trees,
stable positions, partial publication, semifinal-loser routing, light/dark
readability and disabled future-card interaction. The current phone run and
obsolete native release were stopped at the reader's request.
