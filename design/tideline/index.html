In place

The vote docks under the move row, over the board's own footer. It arrives once, after thirty seconds of watching, and it is dismissible. Dismissing does not kill it: it drops to the slim rail and stays there until move 20, so a late guess is always one tap away.

  • Tap either half of the pill to vote. Tap again to switch.
  • The × collapses; it never removes.
  • Numbers keep moving whether you voted or not.

  • Three hit targets; the middle one is the least fun to tap
